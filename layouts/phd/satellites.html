Close approaches

Your browser screens the objects shown for pairs that pass close to each other. A rough approximation from public orbit data, not a collision warning: these orbits are often off by a kilometre or more, and operators work with better orbits and collision probabilities. Pairs that move together (docked, or fragments still side by side) and pairs of two Starlink satellites are left out.
