Live maps from swisstopo

The Switzerland page shows live maps from swisstopo, the Swiss federal mapping office. Your browser loads the maps, the 3D terrain, place search and height profiles from their servers, which see your IP address, as any website you visit does. Some layers come from other public offices and load only when you turn them on: the avalanche bulletin (SLF), the weather stations (MeteoSwiss), and the snow cover seen from space (NASA).
