Live maps from swisstopo

The Switzerland page shows live maps from swisstopo, the Swiss federal mapping office. Your browser loads the maps, the 3D terrain, place search and height profiles from their servers, which see your IP address, as any website you visit does. Some layers come from other services and load only when you turn them on: the avalanche bulletin (SLF), the weather stations and the rain radar (MeteoSwiss), the temperature and cloud maps (Open-Meteo), routes along trails (BRouter, which receives the points you tap), the snow cover seen from space (NASA), and for the Visitors sheet the next trains from St. Gallen and from Zürich Airport (transport.opendata.ch) and the weather there and on the Säntis (MeteoSwiss).
