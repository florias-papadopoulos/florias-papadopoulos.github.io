Everything below is worked out by your own browser, on this page: none of it is stored or sent anywhere, apart from the IP lookup you can start with the button.

This site runs no analytics. This is what I, as its owner, could see about your visit, shown to you only.

Where you are connecting from

My web server gets your public IP address with every request, and a free lookup turns it into a rough location and your internet provider. Your browser cannot see that address itself, so to show it to you this page has to ask an outside service.

Pressing it sends your IP address to ipapi.co, a third-party service. Nothing is sent before you press it.
