What I could see, if I wanted to

My counter is a small server of mine on Cloudflare. A real tracker would save everything below with one line of code; the button asks mine to send it straight back to you instead, and it forgets it. Nothing happens until you press it.

Optional: let this page remember your browser, the way a tracking cookie would. It keeps a random id, a visit count and the date of your last visit, in your browser only, until you press Forget me.

What reaches my server with every visit

Every counted page view sends my counter one small request, and Cloudflare attaches all of this to it automatically. My counter keeps only the five totals above and drops the rest. The button just asks it to show you what arrived with your request. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, normal visits send the counter nothing at all; this part still works, because pressing the button is your own request.
