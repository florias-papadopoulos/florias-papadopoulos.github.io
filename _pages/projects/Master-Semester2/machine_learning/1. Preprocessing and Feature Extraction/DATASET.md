# The sensor data

The raw recordings `Participant_1.csv` to `Participant_10.csv` that the notebooks read (about 31 MB each) are not
kept here. They were handed out with the course and match the public smartphone activity dataset of the Pervasive
Systems group at the University of Twente: 10 participants, phones at five body positions (left and right pocket,
wrist, upper arm, belt), seven activities (walking, jogging, sitting, standing, biking, walking up and down stairs).

- Download: https://www.utwente.nl/en/eemcs/ps/research/dataset/ (Physical activity recognition datasets)
- Paper: M. Shoaib, S. Bosch, O. D. Incel, H. Scholten, P. J. M. Havinga, "Fusion of Smartphone Motion Sensors for
  Physical Activity Recognition", Sensors 14(6), 2014. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4118351

To run Notebook 1, put the ten files in this folder. The smaller CSV files here are our own results from them.
