# Music for the games

Background music for the music button on the Games page (`assets/js/games/arcade.js`). All four tracks are from
OpenGameArt.org and are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use, change and host, no payment and no credit required.
They are credited anyway, here and in the music menu.

Changes made to every track: converted to MP3 (112 kbps, stereo, 44.1 kHz) with ffmpeg, loudness evened out
(loudnorm, -16 LUFS), original tags removed and title and author written in. Nothing was cut or trimmed.
