Music in the games: credits
===========================

The music button on the Games page (florias-papadopoulos.github.io/games/) plays
four mixes. Each mix joins several tracks from OpenGameArt.org, all released
under CC0 1.0, a public domain dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
CC0 allows any use, change and hosting, with no payment and no credit required.
The authors are credited here anyway. Thank you to all of them!

What was changed: leading and trailing silence cut, loudness evened out
(ffmpeg loudnorm, -16 LUFS), mixed down to mono, joined with 3 second
crossfades, 1.5 second fade in and out at the ends of each mix, encoded as
MP3 at 72 kbps. Nothing else was edited. Times are where each track starts.
