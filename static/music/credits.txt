Music in the games: credits
===========================

The music button on the Games page (florias-papadopoulos.github.io/games/) plays
six mixes, one per mood. Each mix joins four or five tracks from Jamendo
(jamendo.com), all released by their artists under Creative Commons licences.
Thank you to all of them!

Licences used (no track here has a "no derivatives" licence):
  CC BY 3.0         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  CC BY-SA 3.0      https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
  CC BY-NC 3.0      https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/
  CC BY-NC-SA 3.0   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/
BY asks for credit and a note of what was changed, which is this file. SA asks
that a changed version is shared under the same licence, so each mix that holds
an SA track is offered under that licence (given under each mix below). NC
forbids commercial use: this is a personal, non-commercial website, with no ads,
no payments and nothing for sale, and the music is only played on it.

What was changed: leading and trailing silence cut, loudness evened out
(ffmpeg loudnorm, -16 LUFS), mixed down to mono, joined with 3 second
crossfades, 1.5 second fade in and out at the ends of each mix, encoded as
MP3 at 64 kbps. Nothing else was edited. Times are where each track starts.
The source files are the free MP3 downloads from each track's Jamendo page.
